## 🔍 Strategic Insights & Data Story

1. **Cars are the Overwhelming Casualty Driver (~80%):**  
   Passenger cars account for 3.33 lakh casualties. Enforcing urban speed limits and mandatory seatbelt compliance will generate the highest marginal reduction in injuries.
2. **Seasonal High-Risk Spikes (October & November):**  
   Both years observed distinct seasonal casualty peaks in Oct-Nov (~21,000 casualties/month), triggered by early evening darkness, low visibility, and slippery autumn road surfaces.
3. **Single Carriageway Hazard (74% of Casualties):**  
   Single-lane two-way roads with opposing traffic caused **3.11 lakh casualties** (over 4.5x higher than dual carriageways), indicating a critical need for physical median dividers.
4. **Daylight vs. Nighttime Distribution:**  
   **73% of casualties** occurred during daylight hours due to high commuter congestion, while night collisions had a significantly higher fatality rate.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture & Advanced Excel Features

* **Multi-Table Pivot Caches:** Efficiently aggregated 307,974 records without workbook performance lag.
* **Dynamic `=GETPIVOTDATA` Calculations:** Implemented robust formulas for KPI cards that resist filter shifts and dynamic table resizing.
* **Interactive Slicers & Timeline:** Synchronized multi-table Report Connections for real-time exploratory drill-downs across **Urban/Rural Area**, **Accident Year**, and **Date Hierarchy**.
* **Advanced Charting:**
  * **2D Line Chart with Markers:** Monthly Year-over-Year (CY vs. PY) variance trajectory.
  * **Hierarchy Treemap Chart:** Road surface distribution (Dry, Wet, Snow, Ice).
  * **Custom Doughnut Charts:** Severity and light condition proportions.
  * **Horizontal Bar Chart:** Casualty frequency by road layout.
